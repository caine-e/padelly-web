## Court image

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this website. It is conceptual sports atmosphere, not a photograph of a named venue. The real Padelly interface is layered separately in HTML using existing app screenshots.

Project assets:

- `assets/photography/padel-court-1672.webp`, 1672 × 941, desktop.
- `assets/photography/padel-court-840.webp`, 840 × 473, mobile.

The original PNG remains at `/Users/caine/.codex/generated_images/01a158dd-f7d1-7702-b10b-4fead3fba2f6/exec-ea97997b-3d3a-4122-b084-ccf55e8588b2.png`. Project assets are independently saved and optimized. No external image host is required.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: cinematic background photograph for the independent padel scoring app Padelly website. Create a premium editorial sports photograph, wide landscape 16:9 composition. An authentic empty outdoor padel court, deep forest-green artificial turf and precise white padel court service lines, transparent glass walls and dark slim steel uprights, net across the distant middle, mature Mediterranean pine trees beyond the glass. Late afternoon warm directional sun from upper right, long architectural shadows across the textured green foreground. Camera low to the playing surface, wide lens, realistic photographic detail, subtle film grain, sophisticated slightly desaturated green colour grade with warm natural highlights. Keep the entire left half and upper-left area visually quiet and dark enough for large white editorial headline overlay; concentrate the sunlit net and architecture in the right half. No people. No text, no logos, no advertising, no UI, no devices, no tennis racket. Realistic court geometry, no fantasy court. The atmosphere is athletic, fresh, anticipatory and premium, with enough detail to feel like being on court. Full image, no border.
