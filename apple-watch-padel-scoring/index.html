Padel score app for Apple Watch

Count every padel point from your wrist.

Padelly turns Apple Watch into a focused padel scorekeeper: configure players and rules on iPhone, choose the first serving team, then use large Watch controls to record the match without reaching for your phone.

How it worksCompare scoring formats

Score a match in three steps.

  1. Set up the matchChoose singles or doubles, players, one of five formats, and whether to use optional Fitness tracking.
  2. Choose the serverSelect which team serves first. Padelly keeps the team-level serving rotation and tie-break order moving.
  3. Tap each pointUse the large team areas on Apple Watch. The score, games, sets, and match result update automatically.

Built for live play

Big controls, clear serving, reliable undo.

The live Watch screen keeps the two scoring zones easy to hit during a rally break. Elapsed time and the current score stay visible, while serving information follows the rules of the selected format. If the wrong team receives a point, undo restores the score and serving state—even across a completed game, set, or tie-break.

The live match does not depend on the iPhone connection.

Once a match goes live on Apple Watch, the Watch keeps the complete match state locally after every action. If the iPhone is temporarily unreachable, scoring continues normally. When the match ends, the Watch sends the final result; if the phone is still unreachable, delivery waits safely until it can reconnect.

No Watch available? Padelly offers “Start on iPhone instead,” including the serving-team choice and the same scoring rules.

Formats for a quick set or a full club match.

The launch app includes German Club Match, Classic Full Match, Fast Match, Mini Set Match, and Best of 3 Mini Sets. These presets use Advantage or Golden Point and include standard tie-break or super tie-break behavior where the format calls for it.

Fitness is optional. Privacy is local-first.

Apple Fitness, only if you choose it

Padelly can record the match as a workout and show available heart rate and active energy. Health permission is not required for scoring.

No account or live cloud dependency

Player names, scores, history, and settings stay local. There is no Padelly login, analytics, advertising, tracking, or custom cloud backend.

Apple Watch scoring questions

Do I need my iPhone on court?

Use iPhone to prepare the match. After scoring starts on Watch, a temporary phone disconnection does not stop the match.

Can I undo after a game or set ends?

Yes. Padelly’s bounded undo history restores score and serving details across game, set, and tie-break boundaries.

Does scoring require internet access?

No. Live scoring is local and is never gated on a network connection.

Can I score without Apple Fitness?

Yes. Fitness tracking can be off, unavailable, or denied without affecting scoring or match history.
