About Padelly

An independent padel scoring app built to keep the score clear while the game keeps moving.

Built for the pace of the court

Padelly is an independent, local-first padel scoring and match-tracking app for iPhone and Apple Watch. It is designed around a simple division of attention: set up the match and its rules on iPhone, then keep the score close at hand on Apple Watch. Large point controls, serving information and undo are intended to make scoring useful between points, without asking players to turn the match into a screen task.

What Padelly is for

The product focuses on real match flow. It supports singles and doubles setup, a choice of documented match formats, Advantage or Golden Point where applicable, tie-breaks, local match history and reusable player records. When an Apple Watch is not available, scoring can continue on iPhone. Optional Apple Fitness tracking and private iCloud backup are separate choices, so the score does not depend on either one.

Local by default

Padelly does not require an account or login to score a match. Match and player data remain on the user’s devices unless the user chooses the documented private iCloud backup. The product does not present advertising, analytics or tracking as part of its core experience. The Privacy Policy explains the app, website and support-data handling in more detail.
