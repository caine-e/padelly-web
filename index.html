Your Watch is the on-court remote.

Large team controls record each point, serving information stays visible, and undo restores the score across game, set, and tie-break boundaries. If no Watch is available, start and score on iPhone instead.

Fitness is optional

Track the workout—or just the score.

With your permission, Padelly can record the match as an Apple Fitness workout and show available heart rate and active energy. Scoring works normally when Fitness tracking is off, unavailable, or denied.

  • Health access is requested only when you choose tracking
  • Only a small workout summary is attached to match history
  • Optional backup stays in your private iCloud account

A fourth tab for your game

See the patterns after the match.

Analytics turns completed matches into a clear picture of your game: matches played, win rate, time on court, energy, and form over time.

  • Overview cards for matches, win rate, time on court, and energy
  • Form, personal records, opponents, and partners
  • Point quality and Fitness data when the match contains it

Friendly matches count toward time on court, not win rate. Empty metrics stay clear instead of guessing.
