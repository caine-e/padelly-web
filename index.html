MADE FOR THE MOMENT

From first serve to final score.

Everything important is close when you need it, and out of the way when you do not.

01 / PLAN

Make room for the match.

Choose singles or doubles, players, a format, and the first server. Save a plan and pick it up when you reach the court.

02 / SCORE

Eyes up. Wrist ready.

Large Watch controls record the next point. The server stays visible, and Undo restores the score when a tap goes wrong. You can score on iPhone too.

03 / REVIEW

More than a final number.

The Play home brings recent results and form together. History and Analytics let you look deeper after the match.

YOUR MATCH, YOUR RULES

Pick the format that fits the court.

Choose singles or doubles, your deuce rule, set structure and games per set. From a quick match tiebreak to best of three, the rules are clear before play begins.

A HIDDEN DETAIL

A little gold for the big moments.

For players who discover it, Royalty Mode adds a gold greeting and crowns to set and match wins. The scoring stays exactly the same.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your match stays yours.

No account, ads, tracking, or custom cloud backend. Match and player data stay local, with optional private iCloud backup designed for one iPhone.
