Privacy Policy

Padelly is designed to keep scoring and match data under your control. The app has no account system, tracking, advertising, analytics, or custom cloud backend.

Last updated: July 18, 2026

The short version

Padelly does not collect, transmit, share, or sell your personal data. Core scoring works locally and does not depend on an internet connection or Padelly server.

Match data and player names

Player names, completed match history, scores, settings, and player statistics are stored locally on your iPhone. An active Apple Watch match keeps its live state locally on the Watch so scoring can continue if the phone is unreachable. The developer cannot see or access this information.

Optional iCloud backup

If private iCloud backup is available and you enable it, completed matches, player records, backup-safe settings, and your selected app icon may be stored through your own Apple iCloud account. Active matches, Watch live sessions, and raw Health data are excluded. Padelly has no custom backup server, and the current backup is designed for one iPhone; using multiple iPhones may cause duplicates or conflicts.

Apple Health and Fitness

Fitness tracking is optional. With your permission, Padelly can start and save a workout through Apple Health and show available metrics such as heart rate and active energy. Padelly does not store raw Health samples or full heart-rate streams. A small workout summary—such as duration, calories, average heart rate, status, and a workout reference—may be saved locally with the match. No Health information is uploaded to a Padelly server.

Website preferences

This website stores only your appearance and color-preset choices in your browser’s local storage so they persist between pages and visits. The preferences are not sent to Padelly. The website has no analytics, advertising, cookies, tracking pixels, or accounts.

No third-party tracking

Padelly does not include advertising networks, analytics services, tracking pixels, location access, contacts access, or similar tools. We do not build a profile about you or monitor how you use the app or website.

Children’s privacy

Because Padelly does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If Padelly’s privacy practices change, this page will be updated before the change takes effect. The date above shows the latest substantive revision.

Questions

If you have a privacy question, email support@getpadelly.com or visit Padelly Support.
